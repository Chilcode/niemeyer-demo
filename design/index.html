What to look at

  • The article template matters most. Your lead model runs through it — free value, then a call to action at the end of every post.
  • The two lead paths. Buyer search and Equity Snapshot both need to feel worth filling out.
  • The proof numbers. $7.82M, 27 transactions, 5.0 across 36 reviews, RealTrends top 1.5%.
  • Your hours. Daily 8AM–9PM is a real differentiator, so it's given real estate on the page.

What's still missing

  • Photography. Every image is a grey placeholder. This is the single biggest thing standing between this and a finished site — a real estate site made of grey boxes reads as unfinished no matter how good the type is.
  • Reviews. No testimonials appear anywhere, because we don't have the text of any of your 36 and we won't invent them.
  • Missouri compliance. The footer disclosure and your license number are marked placeholders pending MREC verification.
  • Live home search. Requires an IDX feed from Heartland MLS, which needs your and your broker's signatures.
