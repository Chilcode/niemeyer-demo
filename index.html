How I work

You are not a lead.

I don't buy internet leads and I don't run a call center. My business comes from people who already trust me telling someone they love to call me. That changes what I owe you.

The conversation comes before the house

Before we look at a single listing we talk about the money, the timeline, and what you're actually trying to build. Sometimes that conversation ends with "don't move yet." That's a good outcome.

I stay after closing

Most agents disappear once the commission clears. I track your equity, your rate, and your renewal windows, and I call you when something changes that's worth acting on.

My network is yours

Lenders, inspectors, roofers, title. People I've watched work for years. You get the same list I'd give my own family, because you probably came from someone who is.
