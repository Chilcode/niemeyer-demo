What working with me looks like

Step 1. A casual conversation.

We sit down or hop on a call, and I listen. Your goals, your timeline, your budget. It's free, it takes about 20 minutes, and there's no commitment.

Step 2. A plan built for your situation.

Buying, selling, or both at once, what happens next depends entirely on where you are. You'll leave knowing your real numbers and your actual next step, not a generic checklist.

Step 3. I guide, you decide.

I handle the strategy, the negotiating, and the deadlines. You make the calls that matter.

And throughout all of it, you'll never have to chase me for an update. If something changes, you hear it from me first. If nothing's changed, you'll hear that too. My phone's on daily, 8AM to 9PM, and I'm the one who answers.
